        Which country covers more land?
        
    
        Bigger country? is Mapelago's one-minute size game. You see two countries and tap the one that covers more land. Sizes are total areas, using official figures where they differ from the map outlines, and countries whose size depends on what is counted are left out. Each reveal shows both countries at the same scale. There are ten pairs a day. They start far apart and get closer, so expect a close call or two at the end. Everyone in the world gets the same ten pairs, so you can compare notes with a friend. New pairs arrive at midnight, your time.
        
    
        How to play
        
    
        1. Look at the two countries.
        2. Tap the one that covers more land.
        3. Go through all ten pairs. The gaps shrink as you go.

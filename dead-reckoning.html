      Find five places on a blank map
      
    
      Dead Reckoning is Mapelago's daily map-tapping game. You get the name of a place, and you tap where it is on a world map with no labels. Every place scores up to 100 by how far your pin lands from the real spot, and later places count for more, so a perfect day is 1,000. The first place is a capital or a famous city; the last is a small capital or a little-known city. After each tap you see how far off you were, which way the place lies, and a short fact about it. Everyone gets the same five places, and a new set starts at midnight.
      
    
      How to play
      
    
      1. Read the place name.
      2. Tap the map where you think it is. Zoom in to be exact.
      3. Lock in your pin and see how far off you were.
      4. Do all five, then share your score. Play more for endless practice.

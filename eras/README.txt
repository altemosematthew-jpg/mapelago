Old borders for Mapelago

The files in this folder are simplified copies of five maps from the historical-basemaps project,
maintained by André Ourednik with many contributors:
https://github.com/aourednik/historical-basemaps

Years: 100, 1492, 1815, 1914 and 1945.

Changes made to the originals: regions the source leaves unnamed were removed, small outlying
islands were removed, outlines were simplified, and the result was saved as TopoJSON. The script
that does this is tools/eras/make-eras.mjs in the Mapelago source.

These files are offered under the GNU General Public License, version 3, the same licence as the
originals. The full text is in LICENSE.txt in this folder.

Old borders are approximate. The source describes its own limits; names and outlines for some
years and places are disputed or uncertain.

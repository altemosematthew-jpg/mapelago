          Name a country. Skip the obvious.
          
    
          Fathoms is Mapelago's daily naming game. You get seven prompts, such as a country that borders Germany or a capital city in Africa. Each prompt has many right answers, and you give one. The less obvious your answer, the deeper it goes, up to 1,000 fathoms a prompt and 7,000 a day. Depth follows a fixed rule: among the right answers, the country whose largest city is smallest goes deepest. After each prompt you see every right answer, deepest first. A wrong answer scores nothing, and you can pass on a prompt. Everyone gets the same seven prompts, and new ones arrive at midnight, your time.
          
    
          How to play
          
    
          1. Read the prompt, such as a country that borders Germany.
          2. Type one country that fits. Skip the first one that comes to mind if you can.
          3. Score up to 1,000 fathoms. The country whose largest city is smallest goes deepest.
          4. A wrong answer scores nothing. Pass if you're stuck.
          5. Play all seven prompts, for up to 7,000 fathoms.

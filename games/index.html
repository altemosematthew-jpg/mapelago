All Mapelago games

Seven free daily geography games. Everyone gets the same puzzles, and new ones arrive at midnight, your time. No accounts, no ads; your streaks are saved in your own browser.

Mystery city

A city is hidden somewhere in the world. Slide the tiles on a 4 by 4 board: two matching tiles join into one, and each new tile size (2, 4, 8, 16, 32, 64) earns a clue: hemisphere, continent, climate, city size and language, its country's shape, and the first letter of its name. Moves cost points, so drop a pin as soon as you think you know. You have three pins; a pin within 50 km finds it, and every miss tells you how far off you are, which way to go, and opens the next clue.

Play Mystery city

Mystery country

The same tile-and-clue game, with a country to find. The clues are its hemisphere, continent, coast and borders, languages, the first letter of its capital and, last, its outline. A pin anywhere inside the country finds it.

Play Mystery country

Bigger city?

Ten pairs of cities a day. Tap the one where more people live. The pairs start far apart and get closer, and each reveal shows both numbers.

Play Bigger city?

Bigger country?

Ten pairs of countries a day. Tap the one that covers more land. Each reveal shows both areas and both outlines at the same scale.

Play Bigger country?

Voyage

Ten islands, one geography question on each, each worth more than the last, up to 1,000 points. After a right answer, drop anchor and keep your points or sail on. A wrong answer ends the trip with the points from your last harbor, at islands 3 and 6.

Play Voyage

Fathoms

Seven prompts a day, such as "a country that borders Germany". Every prompt has many right answers and you give one. The less obvious your answer, the deeper it goes, up to 1,000 fathoms. A wrong answer scores nothing.

Play Fathoms

Stamps

Spin the wheel. It picks one of six categories: capitals, borders, languages, size, continents or coasts. Answer right and that category is stamped on today's page; a miss gets one more chance. Fill all six.

Play Stamps

Streaks

Each game keeps its own streak. Every 7 days you play a game earns a streak freeze for it (you can hold two); a freeze covers a day you miss. "Your streaks", at the foot of every game, shows them all and gives you a save code to move them to another device.

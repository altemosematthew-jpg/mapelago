      How has the world map changed since 1800?
      
    
      Logbook is Mapelago's daily history game. Each day brings eight questions about how the map has changed since 1800: which of two events came first, when something happened, what a country used to be called, who it became independent from, and where its capital moved. Some questions put a year on a timeline: slide to your guess and the game tells you how hot or cold you are, from a bullseye to freezing. Land within 5 years and it counts. The questions get harder through the round, and through the week, from easy on Monday to hardest on Sunday. Every fact comes from a source-checked list of independence dates, former names, capital moves and 303 dated changes to the map. Everyone gets the same eight questions, and a new logbook arrives at midnight, your time.
      
    
      How to play
      
    
      1. Answer eight questions. They get harder as you go.
      2. For "Which came first?", tap the event that happened first.
      3. For "When was it?", slide to the year and lock it in. You get two tries, with a hot or cold clue after the first. Within 5 years counts.
      4. Each question is worth up to 100 points, 800 a day. The exact year scores most.
      5. Share your row of results. It never gives the answers away.

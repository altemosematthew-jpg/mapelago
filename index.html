        Find today's mystery city

        Mystery city is Mapelago's daily guess-the-city game. You start with 1,000 points and a board of number tiles. Slide them to merge matching pairs, like 2048. Each new tile size you make, up to 64, earns a clue: hemisphere, continent, climate and coast, city size and language, its country's shape, then the first letter and length of the city's name. Every move costs 6 points, so each clue is a small bet, and each new tile size adds half its value back: a 16 adds 8. When you think you know it, drop a pin. You get three, and a pin within 50 km finds the city. A miss costs 50 to 150 points, less the closer it lands. It tells you how far off you were, which way to go, whether you're getting warmer, and opens the next clue for free. Stuck on a city you don't know? Ask the turtle where in its country it is, for 80 points. Short on time? Pick Quick mode before your first move: no tiles, one clue to start, a new clue after each missed pin, and five pins. Your result shows the sum and up to three stars. Everyone plays the same city, and a new one arrives at midnight, your time.

        How to play

        1. Slide the tiles. Two matching tiles merge into one worth double.
        2. Each new tile size, up to 64, unlocks a clue and adds half its value. Every move costs 6 of your 1,000 points.
        3. When you have a guess, drop a pin on the map. You get three.
        4. A pin within 50 km finds the city. A miss costs 50 to 150 points, less the closer it lands, and shows the distance, an arrow toward it, whether you're warmer or colder, and opens the next clue.
        5. Or pick Quick mode: one clue to start, one more after each missed pin, five pins. Find it on pin 1 for 1,000 points, pin 2 for 800, pin 3 for 600, pin 4 for 400 or pin 5 for 200.
        6. Share your result: your stars, your clues and how close each pin came, never the city.

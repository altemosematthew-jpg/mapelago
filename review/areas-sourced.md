# Sourced country areas

**Source for every row:** UN Statistics Division, Demographic Yearbook 2023, Table 3, surface area in km2. Supplied by Duet, 27 Nov 2026. 194 countries.

**In use from No. 17** in Mystery country, Fathoms, Stamps, Voyage and Bigger country? (rounds before No. 17 keep the figures they were played with). The table itself lives in `site.js` (`MXAREA`).

**Not in the source (current value kept, unsourced):** Sudan (SD), Kosovo (XK), Taiwan (TW).

## Rules applied
1. Two countries within 2% of each other are never compared in any game.
2. For Argentina, China, Denmark, France, India, Morocco, Norway, Pakistan, Serbia, Ukraine and the United States, a comparison is also left out when the answer could flip under the other common definition (taken as the range between this figure and the one used before).
3. Differences of more than 10% from the old figure that the notes do not explain are listed in `facts-to-check.md`.

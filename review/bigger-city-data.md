Source: Duet's check of 720 of the game's 730 cities against official sources (November 2026), pasted into the
request for this change. From No. 14 (Fri 27 Nov 2026) the game uses the checked figure for every stale or metro
line, shows its year on the reveal and in the result list, uses today's English names, and leaves out the places
below. Nos. 1 to 13 keep exactly the cities, figures and pairs they were played with (tests/golden.json). The
table itself is the `FIX` and `DROP` lists in the `bigcountry-city` block of bigger.html.

Pool from No. 14: 671 cities (730 less 59). Checked figures used: 374. A day is re-dealt if any pair is within 10%.

## Data notes found while writing these
- **No. 49 Iztapalapa** is a borough of Mexico City, not a separate city: players looking for "a city" may find this unfair.
- **No. 46 Macau** is listed with country "Macao"; it's a special administrative region of China.
- **No. 72 Qarchak** is obscure even for a hard day; consider swapping it.
- **No. 43 Zanzibar** is listed as "Zanzibar"; the city is usually called Zanzibar City (its old quarter is Stone Town).

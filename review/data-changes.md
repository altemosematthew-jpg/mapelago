# Mapelago data changes for review (round 2, stage 2)

Nothing here was checked against a live source: this session cannot open web pages. Every change below is one I am sure of from general reference knowledge; anything less sure is listed under **Flagged, not changed** for your fact-check.

## How the changes are applied

- The original country table in each game file is left exactly as it was. A small table of corrections sits right after it and is applied only from puzzle **No. 14** onward (`FROM=14` in each file).
- **Set `FROM` to the day after you merge**, so nobody's already-played day changes under them. Days before it keep the old facts.
- The same country table is used by: Mystery country, Bigger country?, Voyage, Fathoms and Stamps. Every change applies to all five.

## Flagged, not changed (please fact-check)

- **Burkina Faso:** whether French is still an official language after the constitutional change (my understanding: it became a working language). Fula (Peul) is a national, not official, language.
- **Guinea:** Fula (Peul) listed beside French; it is a national language, not official.
- **Sri Lanka capital:** the table says Colombo; the official capital is Sri Jayawardenepura Kotte (Colombo is the commercial capital). Both are now accepted in Fathoms.
- **Bolivia** (Sucre vs La Paz) and **Eswatini** (Lobamba vs Mbabane): two-capital countries; the table names one. Both accepted in Fathoms.
- **Larger countries' areas:** all come from the map outlines and run a few percent under the standard figures (for example Ukraine 570,137 vs about 603,550; United States 9.44 M vs 9.83 M). Changed only where more than 15% off (Somalia, Morocco). A full replacement with official figures should come from your fact-check, all at once, so comparisons stay consistent.
- **Vatican City** area is stored as 1 km² (actual 0.44–0.49); harmless, as it is the smallest either way.
- **United States official language:** Stamps marks English as official. A March 2025 executive order designated English the official language, so this now looks correct; worth confirming.
- **City data (Bigger city?, Voyage):** mixed population definitions (Buenos Aires metro vs Paris city proper), districts listed beside their parent city (Queens, Kowloon, Istanbul districts, Çankaya, Gustavo A. Madero), and likely bogus entries: Pasragad Branch (Iran), Budta, Camayenne, Chakwama, Kampung Baru Subang, Guli, "Tanda" (Tanta?), Malingao, Nowrangapur (district figure), Nur-Sultan 345k (now Astana, about 1.3 M). Not changed: they need a source.
- **Voyage grammar:** "capital of Philippines" should read "capital of the Philippines" (and similar "the" countries).
- **Fathoms depth data:** 40 countries have no largest-city figure, so Vatican City is the deepest answer to about 13% of prompts. Needs data, not a guess.
- **Mystery city clues** list "main languages" (spoken), for example Uzbek and Russian for Tashkent. That is about speech, not official status, so it was left as is.

Notes from Duet: Harare, Tokyo, Oslo and Astana also appear as Part 1 Block B events; don't ask the same rename twice on one day. One source only, so ask with care or skip: Modimolle; Chernyakhovsk, Korolyov, Gatchina, Dimitrovgrad (Russia), Sarov and Sochi. Kahramanmaras and Sanliurfa kept their old names with an honorific added; Niteroi's 1835 rename was a spelling change; Kota Kinabalu is 1967; there are two Dimitrovgrads (Russia and Serbia), so always say which. Left out as unsettled: Nauru's name change, Myanmar's capital move and Equatorial Guinea's move to Ciudad de la Paz.

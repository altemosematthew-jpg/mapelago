        Ten islands, ten geography questions
        
    
        Voyage is Mapelago's daily trivia trip. There are ten islands, each with one geography question: capitals, outlines, borders, a spot on the globe, which city has the most people, languages, money, highest peaks, phone codes, web addresses, which side of the road people drive on, or landlocked countries. Each island is worth more than the last, up to 1,000 points. After every right answer you choose: drop anchor and keep your points, or sail on. A wrong answer ends the trip and leaves you with the points from your last harbor. You get one spyglass a day, which takes away two wrong answers. Everyone gets the same ten questions, and a new voyage sets sail at midnight, your time. When your trip is over, Play more gives you endless practice questions that never change your score or streak.
        
    
        How to play
        
    
        1. Answer the island's question. Your one spyglass a day removes two wrong answers.
        2. After a right answer, choose: drop anchor to keep your points, or sail on.
        3. Each island is worth more than the last, up to 1,000 points.
        4. A wrong answer ends the trip. You keep the points from your last harbor, at island 3 or 6.
